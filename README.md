🔥 Hot & Cold Number Analysis

Users can analyze numbers according to their historical frequency.

Hot numbers are numbers that have appeared more frequently within a selected historical dataset.

Cold numbers are numbers that have appeared less frequently.

This can help users explore different lottery-selection strategies based on historical statistics.

⏳ Overdue Number Analysis

Lotto Champ can also help identify numbers that have not appeared for a relatively long period.

An overdue number can be interesting from a statistical-analysis perspective, but it is important to understand that being overdue does not mean a number is mathematically guaranteed to appear in the next draw.
